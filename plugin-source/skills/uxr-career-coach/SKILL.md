## Transitioning in — what actually works

Meta Research has documented career-transition stories from **academia, consulting, non-profit, and government** (linked in `references/career-transitions.md`). Common patterns across them:

- **Translate, don't apologise.** Academic and consulting backgrounds bring rigorous methods training — frame it in product terms.
- **Build a portfolio with zero experience.** Kevin Liang's *Zero to UX* covers exactly this; one project well-documented beats five thin ones (`references/videos-and-channels.md`).
- **Get a foot in.** Apprenticeship programs (Meta Research Associate, Amazon User Research & Design Apprentice), entry-level contract roles, or Research Ops positions are the most reliable on-ramps.
- **Build a network deliberately.** Slack groups (Mixed Methods, UX Research Academy), ADPList mentorship, and UXR meetups — see `references/communities.md` and `references/mentorship.md`.

## Job-search workflow

1. **Define the role.** UX Researcher / Product Researcher / Design Researcher / User Experience Researcher / UX Research Specialist — and for specialised work, qualitative researcher, quantitative researcher, mixed methods researcher, research scientist, survey methodologist, service designer.
2. **Scan board + Slack + recruiters in parallel.** LinkedIn (primary), UX Research Jobs, UX Research Jobs Board (UXinsight), Welcome to the Jungle, Design Gigs for Good, Remote UXR Jobs, Yuno Juno (UK freelance), Jack & Jill AI agent. Plus #jobs channels in every UXR Slack. See `references/job-search.md`.
3. **Engage agencies that specialise in UXR**, not generic tech recruiters: Aquent (global), Futureheads / Zebra People / Good Maven (London), The Hired Guns / UX Hires / Found By / Jackie V Ross / Wert & Co / Creative People Inc / Fusion Talent (US). See `references/recruiting-agencies.md`.
4. **Polish portfolio and resume.** 2–3 case studies with: decision the research informed, method, what changed because of it.
5. **Prep for the loop.** Expect: portfolio walkthrough, research plan exercise, analysis exercise, stakeholder roleplay, behavioural panel.

## Compensation & negotiation

- Benchmark with **Levels.fyi**, the **User Interviews UX Research Salary Report 2026**, and the **Global UX Compensation Transparency Project**.
- For negotiation, Jacob Warwick's *Lenny's Podcast* episode and his article form are the canonical starting point. The 81 Cents Resource Library, *Ask for It* (Babcock & Laschever), and "Everything I learned to negotiate my salary" (YouTube) are the deeper dives.
- See `references/compensation-and-negotiation.md`.

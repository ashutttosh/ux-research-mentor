# Source guide

**The UX Research Resource Guide**, last updated April 2026.

A curated collection of resources for UX researchers at any stage — from getting into the field to finding work, building your practice, and negotiating compensation.

Maintained by **Tarah Srethwatanakul**, a Staff Researcher with 10+ years of research experience across fintech (Monzo), big tech (Meta) and independent consulting.

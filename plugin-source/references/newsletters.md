# Newsletters & Substacks

The 6 newsletters from the source guide, with what each covers and why Tarah recommends them.

## AI Customer Research — Caitlin Sullivan
- URL: https://aicustomerresearch.substack.com/  (course: https://maven.com/caitlin/aianalysis  ; companion Substack: https://customerculture.substack.com/)
- Author: Caitlin Sullivan — former Head of UXR at Spotify Business/Soundtrack; has trained teams at Canva, YouTube, Maze.
- Substack: practical AI-for-customer-research workflows readable in under 20 minutes; over 5,000 subscribers.
- Companion *Customer Culture* covers customer-discovery tips, startup case studies, tools.
- Course: **Claude Code for Customer Insights** on Maven — self-paced walkthrough videos, templates, guides delivered weekly. Based on 2,000+ hours testing AI workflows. Teaches reliable AI analysis across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.
- Free Substack; paid course separate.
- The source-guide article most recommended: her piece on **using AI in research analysis**.

## Research Toolbox — Saeideh Bakhshi
- URL: https://saeidehbakhshi.substack.com/  (personal: https://saeideh.com/)
- Author: Saeideh Bakhshi — quant UXR leader and computational social scientist, currently at OpenAI; previously Meta, Google, Block, Yahoo.
- Covers career strategies in research, data and UX research, working in industry, transitioning from academia, breaking into UXR.
- **Quant Research Toolbox custom GPT**: a free GPT-based coach/mentor for quantitative UXRs and aspiring quant UXRs.
- Notable articles: "Introducing Quant Research Toolbox GPT," "Quant Skills for Qual Researchers," "Boost Your Quant Skills," senior research archetypes piece, **survey sampling methods deep-dive** (cited by Tarah).
- Audience: aspiring/practicing quant UXRs, hiring managers, qual researchers expanding quant fluency.
- Free.

## User Weekly — Jan Ahrend
- URL: https://www.userweekly.com/
- Curator: Jan Ahrend, UX Research Lead at YouTube.
- Frequency: weekly email roundup.
- Curates the best UXR articles and podcasts from hundreds of sources — answers "what mattered in user research this week."
- Audience: practicing UXRs who want trend and methodology coverage without monitoring sources themselves.
- Companion: **User Weekly podcast** — bi-weekly, 10–15 min episodes covering Jan's top three picks.
- Notable issues: "Increase Impact with an Internal UXR Newsletter," "Scaling UX Research," "Define your UX Research team's mission, vision and goals," "Systems UX Research," "What's happening to UX Research right now?"
- Free.

## NN/g Newsletter — Nielsen Norman Group
- URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/subscribe/
- Content: latest NN/g articles on usability, design, UX research.
- Audience: working UX professionals across research and design.
- One-way announcement list from alertbox@nngroup.com via MailChimp.
- Privacy: password-protected list; email not sold.
- Free; nngroup.com has free articles, training, UX Certification info.

## UXinsight Newsletter
- URL: https://uxinsight.org/subscribe-uxinsight-newsletter/
- Run by UXinsight (Europe's largest UXR community).
- Frequency: monthly.
- Content: stories, insights, event news, conferences/meetups/trainings, plus latest articles and templates.
- Audience: European UXR community + global readers tracking conferences and methodology content.
- Companion: UXinsight Festival (April 20–22, 2026, Leiden) and event listings at https://uxinsight.org/ux-research-events/
- Free.

## The UX Life Chose Me Newsletter — Penelope Rance
- URL: https://medium.com/the-ux-life-chose-me
- **Status: completed** — 50 monthly issues across 4 years; archive lives on Medium. No new editions.
- Audience: UX researchers and ResearchOps practitioners.
- Content: curated UXR articles, videos, podcasts not surfaced elsewhere; recurring interview-with-a-researcher feature about career journeys.
- Notable issues include the ResearchOps community careers report.
- Past issues remain accessible.

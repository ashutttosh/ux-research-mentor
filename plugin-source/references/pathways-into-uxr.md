# Pathways into UX Research

The on-ramps the source guide highlights, with what each program actually offers.

## Easier-to-enter roles

### UX Research Participant Recruitment (ResearchOps)
- Operational role; the most reliable on-ramp to learn how studies actually work end-to-end before transitioning to a researcher seat.

### Entry-level Contract Role (e.g. Research Assistant)
- Companion read: **Aona Yang — Pros and cons of contracting** (see `career-transitions.md`).
- Big-tech contracts give you access, named-brand portfolio, faster hiring bar — but lower pay, limited benefits, no equity, and uncertain FTE conversion.

## Apprenticeship programs

### Meta Research Associate Program (RAP)
- URLs: https://www.facebookrap.com/  (program-run site)  ;  https://www.metacareers.com/careerprograms/research  ;  https://www.metacareers.com/careerprograms/pathways
- Who it's for: candidates with research aptitude but outside Meta's traditional UXR hiring paths — non-traditional educational/professional backgrounds, career switchers, under-represented groups in tech research.
- **Duration: 6 months**, June through December. Starts with 2-week Design Camp onboarding (shared with Design and Content Strategy disciplines).
- **Status: Full-Time Employees** with the title "Research Associate" from day one — not interns; full Meta employee benefits.
- Mentorship structure: every associate paired with a dedicated mentor responsible for growth throughout the program.
- Outcome: at end of 6 months, associates go through allocation to a permanent UXR team based on skills, interests, and business need; priority placement to teams worked with during the program. Most associates convert into permanent UXR roles.
- Application: historically opens early December for the following June cohort. Form at https://www.facebookrap.com/apply-now. 2026 deadlines check live page.
- Cost: none — paid full-time employment.

### Amazon User Experience Design & Research Apprentice Program
- URLs: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/amazon-program-helps-employees-move-into-ux-research-and-design  ;  https://hiring.amazon.com/why-amazon/career-advancement/apprenticeships-certifications
- Who it's for: candidates with affinity for design and research from non-traditional backgrounds; initially designed for existing Amazon employees moving into UX, but external apprentice cohorts have also been recruited via SVC partnership.
- **Duration: ~12 months full-time.**
- Structure: ~7 months instructor-taught curriculum-based training led by Amazon designers/researchers + School of Visual Concepts (SVC, Seattle) — roughly 10 hours/week instruction, 30 hours/week structured homework. Then ~4 months on-the-job training embedded on Amazon product teams (Prime Video, Alexa, AWS, Amazon Fashion).
- **Pay:** paid apprenticeship (~$37k–$62k annualized for the apprenticeship year per Glassdoor; sponsors in registered apprenticeships typically pay 60–70% of entry-level design salary).
- Outcomes: ~96% of one reported cohort converted to full-time design/research roles at Amazon.
- Cost to apprentice: none — paid program, training included.
- Application: cohort-based; check Amazon Jobs and SVC's apprenticeship page (https://www.svcseattle.com/apprenticeships) for current intake.

## Certifications and courses

### Google UX Design Professional Certificate (Coursera)
- Program URL: https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-ux-design  (also: https://grow.google/certificates/ux-design/)
- The dedicated UXR course inside the certificate: **"Conduct UX Research and Test Early Concepts"** — https://www.coursera.org/learn/conduct-ux-research (Course 4 of 7).
- Who it's for: career-changers and beginners targeting entry-level UX design roles — no prior degree or experience required.
- Duration: self-paced; most learners finish the full 7-course certificate in 3–6 months. Course 4 runs roughly 4–5 weeks at suggested pace.
- **Cost:** $49 USD/month after a 7-day free trial (US/Canada); financial aid available; free audit possible for individual courses but graded work and certificate require subscription.
- What Course 4 teaches: writing a research plan (background, goals, questions, KPIs, methodology, participants, script); planning and running moderated and unmoderated usability studies; affinity-diagramming observations into themes; presenting findings persuasively; iterating designs on research.
- Limitations: certificate is design-led; Course 4 is the only fully research-focused module. Useful as entry credential, less differentiated for candidates with prior research training.

### Interaction Design Foundation — User Researcher Pathway
- Pathway URL: https://www.interaction-design.org/courses?learningPath=user_researcher
- Flagship course: **"User Research – Methods and Best Practices"** — https://www.interaction-design.org/courses/user-research-methods-and-best-practices  (~30 hours over ~6 weeks; no hard deadlines).
- Curriculum: full toolkit of research methods + organizational skill of making research matter (research ops, stakeholder influence, prioritization).
- Certification: courses are expert-graded; industry-recognized Course Certificates suitable for LinkedIn/resume.
- **Cost (late 2025/2026):** annual membership rather than per-course — ~$264/year standard, ~$60/year student, ~$96/year professional discount. Monthly options (~$16/month) without mentor; mentor-tier higher.
- Who it's for: aspiring/practicing UX researchers wanting structured curriculum; designers expanding into research. Self-paced — works alongside a full-time job.
- What you get: access to full IxDF catalog (40+ courses), community features, job-matching, designer tool discounts, templates, User Researcher pathway certificate trail.
- vs. Google: deeper on research-only content, less brand-name recognition, but a more legitimate fit for someone explicitly targeting UXR.

# Job search

The 9 job boards from the source guide, with what each is actually best at.

## LinkedIn (primary)

Most UXR jobs are posted on LinkedIn.

**Relevant search terms:**
Product Researcher · UX Researcher · User Experience Researcher · Design Researcher · User Researcher · UX Research Specialist

**Search terms for related / more specialised roles:**
Qualitative researcher · Quantitative researcher · Mixed methods researcher · Research scientist · Survey methodologist · Service designer

## Job boards

### Jack & Jill AI
- URL: https://www.jackandjill.ai
- Two-sided conversational AI platform: Jack (candidate-facing career agent) + Jill (employer-facing AI recruiter).
- Free for job seekers; Jack scans ~15 million jobs daily and surfaces hyper-personalized startup/tech roles.
- Focus: startup and tech roles (engineering, product, design, marketing, ops); UXR roles appear within broader product/design listings.
- Pitches direct intros to hiring managers, interview coaching, salary benchmarking instead of resume-and-apply.
- Geographic focus: primarily US, with expansion plans (TechCrunch reported $20M seed, Oct 2025); London/EU traction noted.
- Candidate network: ~100k engineers, marketers, salespeople, operators.

### Welcome to the Jungle (formerly Otta)
- URL: https://www.welcometothejungle.com  (app: https://app.welcometothejungle.com)
- French-headquartered employer-branding + job platform; acquired London-based Otta in Jan 2024.
- Curated tech/startup roles with rich company profiles, salary insight, diversity data, editorial company takes.
- Strong UXR presence: live listings observed have included Google Staff UXR, GitLab UXR, Harvey Head of UXR, Wander Head of UXR, Roku UXR, Hinge Lead UXR, Lime Staff UXR, Okta Staff UXR, Zipline UXR Intern, Reveleer UXR.
- Free for candidates; preferences-based matching (skills/values/role-type).
- Geographic focus: UK, US, EU.

### UX Jobs Board (uxjobsboard.com)
- URL: https://www.uxjobsboard.com
- Specialist UX board since 2008; manually screens every posting.
- Disciplines: Interaction Designers, Service Designers, Experience Designers, UI, UX Researchers, Business Analysts.
- Employer-paid tiered packages with money-back guarantee.
- Global reach; testimonials from Apple, BBC, BMW, Yahoo, Microsoft.
- Free for job seekers.
- Bonus: maintains curated list of UX staffing/recruitment agencies as a sub-resource.

### Design Gigs for Good
- URL: https://www.designgigsforgood.org
- Volunteer-run, free international board for design × social-impact roles.
- Disciplines: design/user research, service design, interaction/UX/IA, product design, communication/graphic, illustration, content design.
- Postings distributed via Google Group + Slack community (joinable from site).
- Community functions beyond the board: career advice, feedback, events.
- Roles skew nonprofit, civic tech, public interest, education — many contract/remote.

### Remote UXR Jobs (paixon.io)
- URL: https://paixon.io/senior_remote_uxr_list
- Curated list, not a search engine — focused on remote-only or remote-mostly senior UXR roles (typically 3+ years).
- Updated manually by Ben; no signup or paywall.
- Geographic focus: global remote, US-heavy.
- Hand-filtered antidote to noisy aggregator boards.

### YunoJuno (yunojuno.com)
- URL: https://www.yunojuno.com
- UK-founded freelancer management system; marketplace of 120,000+ vetted freelancers (remote or onsite).
- Strong UX Researcher category with day-rate transparency; 2024 UX discipline average ~£425/day, top 10% ~£675/day.
- Free for freelancers (no platform commission); clients pay platform + markup fees.
- Handles contracts, IR35/compliance, payments, real-time reporting end-to-end.
- Geographic focus: UK primary, expanding EU and global.
- Recognition: Leader in Everest Group FEMS PEAK Matrix 2026.

### UX Research Jobs (uxresearchjobs.com)
- URL: https://www.uxresearchjobs.com
- Niche aggregator dedicated solely to UXR roles (global, remote-friendly).
- Live but thin compared to larger boards — primarily a listing feed; minimal editorial depth.
- Free for candidates.
- Useful as focused supplement to broader boards; expect overlap with LinkedIn/Indeed feeds.

### UX Research Jobs Board — UXinsight
- URL: https://uxinsight.org/ux-research-jobs/
- Run by UXinsight (Netherlands-based UXR community/conference).
- Postings free for employers for 2 months, paid upgrades for promoted visibility.
- Truly UXR-only — not mixed with product design or UI.
- Global reach with stronger European representation than US-centric boards.
- Tied into UXinsight's broader resources (conferences, training, ambassador program).

## Freelance / project-based remote research

### Askable (askable.com)
- URL: https://www.askable.com
- Australian (Brisbane HQ) AI research platform; combines participant recruitment with freelance researcher network.
- "Certified Askable Researcher" network of 750+ freelancers, avg 10+ years experience; reported earnings up to $5,000 per project.
- Project flow: notified of open briefs → apply → video brief with client → recruit participants on Askable's panel → run sessions → deliver insights.
- Free for researchers to apply/certify; clients pay per-project.
- Geographic focus: global remote, strong APAC and US.
- Notable: triple-verified participant panel + AI agents for moderation/synthesis built into platform.

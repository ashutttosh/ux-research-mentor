# Recruiting agencies with UXR talent specialists

From the source guide. Each entry: who they are, geo, what they do well.

## Global

### Aquent (aquent.com)
- URL: https://aquent.com  (UK: https://aquent.co.uk)
- Global creative/marketing/tech staffing; one of the canonical **Google contract** pipelines.
- 400+ Aquent contractors placed across 15 Google divisions (Play, Workspace, Nest, Maps, etc.).
- Roles include UX Researchers, UX Designers, Producers, Video Editors, Content Strategists, PMs.
- Both contract and full-time placements; W2 contractors in the US get benefits.
- Geographic focus: US, UK, EU, AU — truly global.
- Well-trodden route for breaking into Big Tech UXR via contract-to-perm.

## London / UK

### Futureheads (wearefutureheads.com)
- URL: https://www.wearefutureheads.com  (note: domain is wearefutureheads.com, not futureheads.co.uk)
- Founded 2009; ~40-person team in Farringdon, London.
- B Corp certified; specialisms: creative, digital, design, engineering, project/programme, data, marketing.
- Long-standing UK UX presence; sponsors/runs UX networking events.
- Both permanent and contract recruitment.
- Geographic focus: London + UK-wide; some EU work.

### Zebra People (zebrapeople.com)
- URL: https://zebrapeople.com
- Founded 2001 in Hoxton — claims to be Europe's first specialist digital recruiter.
- B Corp; specialisms: UX, Service Design, Research, UI, Product Design, Tech.
- Builds entire UX teams from scratch as well as individual placements (Junior to CXO).
- Permanent + contract; reported CV:interview 2:1 and interview:placement 3:1.
- Geographic focus: London-centric, UK-wide.
- Dedicated UX & Service Design Jobs page consistently lists UXR roles.

### Good Maven (goodmaven.com)
- URL: https://goodmaven.com  (note: .com, not .io)
- Design recruiting + coaching agency founded by Meg, the first international design recruiter at Meta (helped scale Meta design from 4 to ~700 across 13 countries).
- Team includes ex-Meta, ex-Microsoft, ex-Instagram recruiters and leaders.
- Disciplines: product design, content design, UX research, AR/VR, product management.
- Network of 50,000+ connections; clients include Meta, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google, eBay, Siemens, Sanity.io, Revolut.
- Combines placement with career coaching (CV, portfolio, interview prep).
- Geographic focus: US, UK, Europe.

## United States

### The Hired Guns (thehiredguns.com)
- URL: https://thehiredguns.com
- NYC-based digital talent agency founded ~2000 by Allison Hemming.
- Network of ~40,000 opted-in digital "visionaries."
- UX/UI is a named discipline; current listings observed: UX Research Lead, UX Researcher, Quantitative UX Research Assistant, Director of UX Research, Associate Creative Director (UX), UX PM.
- Engagement types: permanent, project, consulting, corporate training/L&D.
- Geographic focus: US-wide, NYC-anchored.

### UX Hires (uxhires.com)
- URL: https://uxhires.com
- NYC-headquartered (20–49 employees); UX/CX/Marketing specialist.
- Roles: Product Designers, UX/UI, AI Designers, UX Researchers, UX Writers/Content Designers, UX Leads/Principals, PMs, Product Marketers, Front-End/Full-Stack Devs.
- Clients range from startups to Fortune 100.
- Both full-time placement and contractor/staff augmentation; standard recruiting fee model (~20–30% of base for perm, hourly markup for contract).
- Geographic focus: US/Canada primary; EU and LATAM partners.

### Found By (foundby.co)
- URL: https://www.foundby.co
- Boutique **design leadership** recruiting; 10+ years.
- Founders/team include ex-LinkedIn, ex-Airbnb, ex-Squarespace senior design recruiters.
- Specialises in design leadership roles (UXR/Design Director, Head of Research, VP) — not high-volume IC placement.
- Service line beyond placement: hiring playbooks, structured interviewing, levelling/career frameworks, design brand strategy, DEI strategy, recruiting-leader advising.
- Geographic focus: US primary.
- Not to be confused with UK-based "Found By Few" (foundbyfew.com), a separate D&I-focused product/design firm.

### Jackie V Ross — Jackie Velasquez-Ross
- URL: https://www.jackievross.com  ;  https://www.linkedin.com/in/jackievross
- **Individual recruiting leader, not an agency** — currently VP, Global Talent Acquisition at Tiny (the holdco).
- Prior: led recruiting at MetaLab; managed Twitter recruiting team sourcing product designers, UXRs, content designers; earlier at Facebook on product design leadership recruiting.
- Specialisation: design + UX research recruiting at senior/leadership levels, especially within design-led product orgs.
- Active mentor — offers portfolio reviews, comp-negotiation coaching, referrals; particularly for women, POC, LGBTQ+ candidates.
- Geographic focus: US/global remote (Tiny portfolio is global).
- Engage via LinkedIn DM rather than standard application flow.

### Wert & Co (wertco.com)
- URL: https://www.wertco.com
- Privately held NYC-based design recruiting firm, founded 1995.
- Small team (2–10); long-standing senior network in design community.
- Recruits across UX, AI software designers, product designers, brand, industrial, creative marketing.
- Services beyond search: advisory consulting on design culture, hiring process, team building, venture consulting.
- Nadya Karyo leads research/business intelligence/strategy practice; Daniel Wert became CEO in 2026 (joining founders Judy and Jeff Wert).
- Geographic focus: US, primarily East Coast / NYC.
- Sweet spot: mid-to-senior creative leadership rather than IC UXR.

### Creative People Inc (creativepeopleinc.com)
- URL: https://www.creativepeopleinc.com
- "Human-first" creative recruiting agency for marketing, design, product.
- 5 divisions across 5 cities (NYC/Brooklyn, LA, SF, Seattle, +); 26-person team; 50,400+ candidates; 310+ clients.
- Contractor division (Seattle + NYC) handles freelance/contract staffing across Marketing, Brand Creative, Product Design.
- Both full-time and contract placements; works heavily with high-growth startups.
- 2024 Inc. 5000 honoree.
- Geographic focus: US (multi-coastal).
- UXR sits within Product Design division rather than as a standalone vertical.

### Fusion Talent (fusiontalent.com)
- URL: https://www.fusiontalent.com
- Executive search — engineering, product, and design leadership.
- Focused on senior/leadership placements (retained search model).
- Geographic focus: US primary.
- For UXR specifically: relevant for Head of Research / VP Research / Design Research Director, not IC UXR.
- Note: similar-named firms exist (Fusion Recruiters in Wisconsin, Talent Fusion, Fusion Talent UK heavy engineering) — these are unrelated.

## Pricing convention (industry norm)
- Perm placements: 20–30% of base salary
- Contractors: hourly markup
- Retained executive search: 25–35%, paid in thirds (Wert & Co, Fusion Talent, Found By's leadership work)

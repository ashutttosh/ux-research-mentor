# Podcasts

The full list from the source guide, with substance from each show's public landing page.

## Mixed Methods
- URL: https://www.mixed-methods.org/episodes
- Host: Aryel Cianflone (UX researcher).
- 31 episodes across 3 seasons; ran 2017–2020. First season passed 12,000 listeners.
- Audience: practicing UXRs curious about methods plus broader career-craft skills.
- Signature guests: Aaron Sedley (Google), Jared Spool, Jane Fulton Suri (IDEO).
- Format: in-person interviews; later seasons broadened from method-specific to research practice and skill development.
- Available on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, RSS feed.

## Dollars to Donuts
- URL: https://portigal.com/podcast/
- Host: Steve Portigal (author of *Interviewing Users*; founder of Portigal Consulting).
- Focus: conversations with leaders of user research inside organisations.
- Audience: UXR managers and leaders building or running research teams.
- Standout recent episodes: Emily Sun (Hipcamp) on engagement and small-team research; Vanessa Arango Garcia (Delivery Hero) on global research community; Sarah Gregory (Coinbase) on research comms; Akshay Verma (Duolingo) on qual in an experimentation culture.
- Full transcripts on the Portigal blog.
- Free.

## NN/g UX Podcast
- URL: https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/nn-g-ux-podcast/id1527196035 (also Spotify, YouTube)
- Host: Therese Fessenden, Senior UX Specialist at Nielsen Norman Group.
- Frequency: bimonthly.
- Audience: UX practitioners across research, design, strategy.
- Topics: AI in UX, conversational design, journey management, accessibility, hiring, UX career.
- Free.

## Awkward Silences
- URL: https://www.userinterviews.com/podcast (episodes at https://podcast.userinterviews.com/episodes)
- **Hosts: Erin May and Carol Guest** (VPs at User Interviews) — note: the source guide's "Erin Carl" is an error; correct names captured here.
- Frequency: biweekly. 198+ episodes; 4.9/5 stars on Apple.
- Audience: UX researchers, PMs, ResearchOps practitioners.
- Topics: qualitative methods, AI in research, ResearchOps, career advice, craft of understanding people.
- Free.

## Brave UX with Brendan Jarvis
- URL: https://thespaceinbetween.co.nz/brave-ux-podcast (Podbean: https://braveux.podbean.com/)
- Host: Brendan Jarvis (founder of The Space InBetween, New Zealand).
- **Status: completed**, 171 episodes over 5 years; final monologue reflects on starting during the pandemic.
- Audience: senior UX, product, design practitioners; thought-leader conversations.
- Standout recent episodes: Lawton Pybus PhD on the UX job market (Feb 4, 2025); Felix Lee on democratising mentorship with ADPList (Jan 7, 2025); two-part Tamara Adlin episode on innovation through UX insights (Dec 2024).
- Free; archive accessible.

## What I Wish I Knew (Twitter Research)
- URL: https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/i-wish-i-knew/id1562383399 (intro: https://blog.x.com/en_us/topics/company/2021/i-wish-i-knew-podcast)
- Run by Twitter Research / @TwitterResearch (now X); two seasons.
- Audience: aspiring researchers and people curious about big-tech research culture.
- Format: hosted by Twitter researchers sharing career journeys, why research matters, team culture.
- Topics: non-traditional paths into UXR, designing/researching in public (Twitter Spaces case study), the Rapid Research program, must-have skills for new researchers.
- Status: post-X-acquisition status unclear; treat as an archive.

## The World of UX with Darren Hood
- URL: https://worldoux.com/ (Apple: https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-world-of-ux-with-darren-hood/id1514004102)
- Host: Darren Hood — 30+ year UX veteran, educator, conference speaker, author.
- Premiered 2020; self-described "no-holds-barred."
- Audience: UX practitioners across experience levels with a strong career-advice tilt.
- Topics: UX methods, discipline trends, career advancement, education choices and pitfalls, UX maturity, leadership challenges.
- Available on Spotify, Apple, Amazon Music, Podbean.

## Quantitude
- URL: https://quantitudepod.org/about/ (Apple: https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/quantitude/id1484406501)
- Co-hosts: Patrick Curran and Greg Hancock, quantitative methodology professors (UNC and UMD).
- 181 episodes; ongoing.
- Audience: academic quantitative researchers and quant-curious UXRs; statisticians, methodologists.
- Topics: time-varying covariates in growth models, power analysis, bootstrapping, latent transition analysis, real-world stats applications.
- Self-described tone: serious stats topics with irreverent humour.
- Free on Apple, Spotify, Audible, YouTube (@quantitudepod); merch on Redbubble.

## Anthro to UX with Matt Artz
- URL: https://anthropologytoux.com/anthro-to-ux-podcast/
- Host: Matt Artz — business/design anthropologist, AI product researcher, founder of Azimuth Labs, adjunct prof at University of Pennsylvania.
- Audience: anthropologists and social scientists transitioning into UX research.
- Format: interviews with anthropologists working in UX about how they transitioned, lessons learned, what they'd change.
- Practical career advice on UXR resumes, portfolios, skill translation.
- Free; widely available (Apple, Spotify, Amazon Music, YouTube).

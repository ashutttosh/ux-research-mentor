## Salary benchmarks — updated for 2026

### Global UX Compensation Transparency Project (Amy Santee)
- URL: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1xaz_n6Iys5H2tALBySI_7nXVBygN3d9moGLriOhj61I/edit
- Landing: https://www.amysantee.com/post/copy-of-global-ux-compensation-transparency-project
- Crowdsourced public Google Sheet maintained by Amy Santee; launched September 2021; data spans 2021–2026.
- ~1,500+ anonymous entries by recent updates.
- Scope is global; full-time, contractor, consulting UX roles; primary coverage research and design, plus strategy, ops, content/writing, management.
- Fields captured: raw base salary and total comp; job title and level; geography; benefits; certifications/degrees/honors; gender, race, ethnic identity; company budget and headcount; equity/stock; bonuses.
- Stated purpose: help underrepresented groups (gender, race, ability, citizenship, caretaker status) reduce information asymmetry in negotiation and promotion.
- Intended as community resource, not a methodologically controlled survey.
- Contributors are encouraged to share granular details including company name.

### User Interviews UX Research Salary Report 2026
- URL: https://www.userinterviews.com/ux-salary-report
- Methodology: combines ~3,000 self-reported records from 2023–2025 State of User Research surveys (1,906 specifically referenced) plus ~17,736 records from Levels.fyi (2023–2025) — roughly 20,000 global UX professionals across Researcher, Designer, ResearchOps titles.
- IC vs. manager pay diverges meaningfully only at director level; senior/principal ICs often match or exceed manager comp.
- Sample drops sharply beyond 7–9 years of experience — under-reporting from senior cohorts.
- **Numbers (USD unless noted):**
  - US UX Researcher base typically $110K–$120K; total comp range $95K (small/low-cost) to $200K+ (large tech, high-cost metros, equity included).
  - US entry-level (0–2 YOE) base $65K–$95K; small-co entry $65K–$80K.
  - UK senior researchers at major tech in London ~£70K–£110K; UK comp ~45–55% of US.
  - Canada researcher median ~CAD 121K (~USD 87K); overall ~50–65% of US.
  - Germany junior/mid ~USD 68K median; senior/manager ~USD 89K.
  - Spain junior/mid ~USD 46K; senior/manager ~USD 65K.
  - Australia/NZ median ~NZD 151K / AUD 216K (~USD 91K).
  - UK UX specialist median ~£66K (~USD 89K at early-2026 exchange).
  - Researchers vs. designers: entry-level UXR slightly higher; mid-career roughly equal; senior gap likely real and meaningful.
- Filters: industry, education level, how research skills were acquired, company size, geography, YOE.
- Underlying data available for download (XLSX, CSV).

## Negotiation resources

### Jacob Warwick on Lenny's Podcast
- URL: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/the-tactical-playbook-for-getting-more-comp
- Episode published March 15, 2026; runtime ~1h 55m.
- **Frame:** treat the job search like an enterprise sales process; the best interview should feel like a discovery call rather than an interrogation.
- **Core claim:** a soft prompt asking whether there is a little more available frequently produces a 20% bump; well-run negotiations average ~40% movement.
- Negotiation starts before the offer arrives; information and timing are the two main sources of leverage.
- Naming a number first risks anchoring low and making your first figure a ceiling; deflect the "What's your comp expectation?" question.
- Product leaders, engineers, designers tend to negotiate worse because thoughtful, introverted, competent people over-index on being reasonable.
- Approach is collaborative not confrontational; focus on understanding the counterparty's motivations and constraints.
- Cited case: Senior PM, advertised $185K–$285K range turned into a $1.1M annual package (close to 4× the high end).
- He claims to have helped clients secure over $1B in additional compensation.
- Negotiate beyond base: equity, sign-on, severance terms, title, scope, reporting line.

### Jacob Warwick — Article / companion content
- Site: https://www.thinkwarwick.com/
- Substack: https://www.execsandthecity.com  (post: "What We Didn't Get To on Lenny's Podcast")
- Reiterates the discovery-call interview frame and enterprise-sales framing of job search.
- Recruiters are signaling agents whose first number is rarely their ceiling.
- Prepare a "value narrative" tied to business outcomes the next employer cares about — not personal need.
- Severance, equity refresh schedule, and title are undervalued levers most candidates skip.
- Multi-offer discipline: real or credible alternative options shift leverage materially.
- He works mostly with senior operators (Director and up), athletes, and entertainment talent; booking via Intro: intro.co/JacobWarwick.

### 81 Cents Resource Library
- URL: https://www.81cents.com/resources
- Founded to help women and underrepresented minorities close the pay gap; later acquired by Rora.
- Track record: 900+ underrepresented employees coached; average pay increase ~18%.
- Library curated from input by 2,000+ hiring managers and recruiters.
- Contents: negotiation scripts, a Script Generator tool, workshop replays, compensation datasets, FAQs, "81 Grants" program for those who can't afford services.
- Flagship paid product: **Market Value Report** — 25-page personalised brief covering base, bonus, equity, 401(k) match, benefits, plus tactical phrasing for the user's specific conversation.
- Sliding-scale pricing + partial/full scholarships.
- Coaching is 1:1: new offers, promotions, raises, severance.
- Audience: women and underrepresented job-seekers/employees.

### "Ask for It" — Linda Babcock & Sara Laschever
- URL: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/6885/ask-for-it-by-linda-babcock-and-sara-laschever/
- See also full entry in `books.md`.
- Core argument: women systematically under-ask; men initiate negotiation ~4× more often.
- **Four-phase program:**
  1. **"Everything Is Negotiable"** — audit what you actually want to change.
  2. **"Lay the Groundwork"** — gather information; define BATNA, reserve value (walk-away), target value (goal).
  3. **"Get Ready"** — pick the right target; decide whether to make the first offer; time the ask. Advocates cooperative "expanding the pie" over zero-sum positioning.
  4. **"Put It All Together"** — practice and role-play to manage emotions, anticipate objections, rehearse the script.
- Signature tactics: anchor with research; frame asks in terms of mutual value; ask diagnostic questions to learn the other side's constraints; use cooperative language while holding firm on the number.
- Companion: *Women Don't Ask* (the research-heavy precursor).

### "Everything I learned to negotiate my salary" — YouTube (tech)
- Best matches:
  - "Everything You Need to Know to NEGOTIATE SALARY (Without Selling Yourself Short)" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vq-56C5YjQ
  - "How to Negotiate a Software Engineer Salary" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t_YorE8eBOw (Aug 2022)
- Framework consistent across these tech talks:
  - Never give the first number; deflect to range questions ("What's the band for this role?").
  - Always negotiate after a written offer is in hand; leverage window is 48–72 hours when competing offers carry the most weight.
  - Move negotiation to phone, not email — pauses and tone reveal recruiter flexibility; follow up by email to document what was agreed.
  - Aim for 10–15% above the initial offer as a default opener; data cited that average bump from simply asking is ~$10K, and ~85% of those who negotiate get more.
  - Negotiate the full compensation surface: base, sign-on, equity (RSU count + refresh), bonus target, start date, vacation, remote/hybrid, title, level, education stipend, relocation.
  - Recruiters as allies — their commission scales with your comp; ask which levers their team is most flexible on.
  - Standard script when receiving an offer: thank you → enthusiasm → state target range with confidence → pause and let silence work.
  - Companies rarely rescind offers over a respectful negotiation; perceived risk much higher than actual risk.

## How `uxr-career-coach` skill uses these

1. **Pull a benchmark** (Levels.fyi → User Interviews 2026 report → Transparency Project).
2. **Determine the band** the user should be in (level × geography × company size).
3. **Prepare the conversation** using Jacob Warwick's frame.
4. For under-negotiated groups, layer in **81 Cents** and ***Ask for It*** frameworks.
5. Equip with **tech-specific tactics** from the YouTube talks above for the actual call.

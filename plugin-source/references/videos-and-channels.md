# Videos and YouTube channels

The video resources from the source guide, with what each channel/video actually covers.

## Aona Yang — "aonatalks" YouTube
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/c/aonatalks  (Medium: https://medium.com/@aonayang)
- Author: Aona Yang — Staff UX Researcher at Google DeepMind (Gemini); previously UX Research Lead/Manager for Gemini and Search at Google; UXR at Airbnb and Sumo Logic.
- Channel active Nov 2020 – Nov 2022; educational UXR videos + career-in-Silicon-Valley content.
- Standout videos referenced by Tarah: **"How to become a UX Researcher"** and her **UX Stakeholder Management** 3-part series.
- Also offers 1:1 mentorship; active on ADPList.
- Free.

## Kevin Liang — Zero to UX YouTube + course
- URLs: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCWJE3xgMkuekX376g3SiwrQ  ;  course: https://zerotoux.com/
- Author: Kevin Liang — career switcher from biology to UXR; worked at Google, Unity, Volkswagen, Uber.
- Channel covers methodologies, leadership tips, career advice, mock interviews.
- Standout videos referenced by Tarah: **free remote research tools** (e.g., "FREE Tools for Remote UX User Research Testing" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n-yB6SazSBs), and **creating a portfolio with zero experience**.
- Companion: Zero to UX Self-Paced Masterclass — Module 1 free; full masterclass paid; built over 5 years.
- Mentorship + resources for early-career UXRs.

## UX Quests — Noam Segal
- URL: see "UX Quests" YouTube + maven.com cohort site.
- Author: Noam Segal — has led UXR at Airbnb, Intercom, Twitter, Wealthfront, Meta, more recently Upwork; PhD Psychology, UIUC; first UXR role on Israeli missile-defence systems.
- Channel and newsletter share UXR content and craft tips.
- Runs a Maven school for UX practitioners.
- Standout video: "How to Get a Job in UX" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l-sn2UtYm_8 ; "Doing UX research with AI" on Dive Club.
- Audience: senior and aspiring UXRs interested in research strategy and AI in research.
- Free YouTube + paid Maven cohorts.

## Learners
- URLs: https://joinlearners.com/  resource hub https://www.researchresources.joinlearners.com/
- Self-described as the largest UX research community in the world.
- Free community-crowdsourced UX research content: practitioner talks and videos from researchers at InVision, Zoom, RBC, Kira Systems, Mailchimp, others.
- Intro to UX Research program: free week-long event (online + in-person) with senior researchers teaching basics.
- Hosts **Research Week** events for both seasoned researchers and newcomers.
- Audience: anyone learning UXR — particularly strong for beginners.
- Free.

## Delta CX — YouTube
- URLs: https://www.youtube.com/c/deltacx and https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCCS3UyqGEXZe8SSFOf0JLpQ (Delta CX Hive)
- Author: Debbie Levitt, MBA, CPIC, ACC — CXO of Delta CX; CX/UX strategist, researcher, service designer.
- 1,000+ videos across the two channels. Original channel: archive from Oct 2019 – July 2023 with 900+ videos. Current/live content: Delta CX Hive.
- Signature show: **"Think Out Loud"** — Monday live show on qualitative UX research planning, IA, and interaction design; Debbie shares her decision-making in real time.
- Companion live podcast (Delta CX Live) covers CX, UX, usability, careers, team improvement.
- Audience: practicing UX/CX researchers who want unfiltered perspectives.
- Free.

## Day-in-the-life / understanding the field

### "A realistic week in my life as a UX Researcher" — Aona Yang
- Likely indexed under a slightly different title on Aona's channel (https://www.youtube.com/c/aonatalks). Browse the channel video list to confirm.
- Channel context: educational UXR vlogs + career content from Nov 2020 – Nov 2022.

### NN/g — "What a UX career looks like today" (2019)
- URL: report at https://www.nngroup.com/reports/user-experience-careers/  ; article at https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-career-advice/
- Format: 90-page free report (2nd edition) based on 2019 research with 722 UX professionals worldwide.
- Coverage: UX specialisation, practitioner backgrounds, skills, responsibilities in today's industry.
- Publisher: Nielsen Norman Group.
- Audience: UX professionals at any stage, hiring managers, educators.
- The article summarizes findings.
- Free download.

### Jan Ahrend's "Humans of Research" series
- Published on LinkedIn (https://www.linkedin.com/in/jan-ahrend / pulse articles).
- Authors: Jan Ahrend with Sabah Haider, PhD.
- Format: interview profiles of UX researchers from non-traditional backgrounds (film, journalism, anthropology) showing how their disciplines feed UXR practice.
- Audience: UXR practitioners and aspirants curious about diverse paths into the field.
- Sample profile: Sabah Haider — Dutch-born, Canadian-raised, BA Politics, journalism career, magazine editor before UXR.
- Companion to User Weekly newsletter and podcast.
- Free.

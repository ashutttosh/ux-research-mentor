# Communities

Where UXRs gather. Gated communities are marked.

## Reddit

### r/UXResearch
- URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/UXResearch/
- Subreddit focused on the practice of UX research; discussions on methods, careers, transitions, tooling, job market.
- Open and free; standard Reddit rules.
- Active peer Q&A; recent post themes center on job-search difficulty, AI's impact on UXR, interview prep, portfolio reviews.
- One of multiple UXR-adjacent subs (also r/userexperience, r/UserExperienceDesign).

## Slack communities (Gated — request access)

### Mixed Methods
- URL: https://www.mixed-methods.org/community1  (Slack invite via https://mixedmethods.herokuapp.com/)
- Audience: UX researchers interested in mixed-methods practice.
- Format: Slack workspace + blog + podcast; channels include #emerging-practices, #uxr-support-info, #researchteamofone.
- Geographic focus: global.
- Cost: free.
- Self-onboard via the community page or Heroku invite link.

### Ethnography Hangout
- URL: https://www.epicpeople.org/new-ethnography-slack/  (join form via https://anthrodesign.com/join-us/)
- Audience: applied ethnographers across industry and academia; no specific background required.
- Format: Slack + listserv, co-stewarded by EPIC, Ethnography Matters, Anthrodesign.
- Cost: free; volunteer-run; onboarding can take 1–2 weeks.
- Questions: natalie at anthrodesign dot com.

### ResearchOps Community
- URL: https://researchops.community/
- Audience: ResearchOps practitioners and adjacent researchers/design ops.
- Format: Slack; global cohort-based onboarding; ~16,000 members across 150+ countries.
- Cost: free.
- Waitlist typically under 4 weeks while admins onboard cohorts. Contact: teamreops@gmail.com.

### IxDA — Interaction Design Association
- URL: https://www.ixda.org/
- Audience: interaction designers globally; broader UX practitioners welcome.
- Format: local chapters (80+ countries, ~150,000 individuals); Slack, LinkedIn, Facebook, X.
- Cost: free; "member if you say you are" model, no dues.
- Note: the non-profit legal entity was dissolved in June 2025; local chapters and the global Slack continue.
- Self-declare via website; access Slack via chapter or global invite links.

### UX Mastery Community
- URL: https://community.uxmastery.com/
- Audience: UX practitioners at all levels, learning-oriented.
- Format: Discourse community + Slack workspace; channels include #resources, #research-methods, #feedback.
- Cost: free.
- Register at community.uxmastery.com, then request Slack invite.

## In-person / digital meetups

### Research Lunch Club
- URL: https://researchlunchclub.com/
- Audience: UX researchers, behavioural scientists, academic researchers.
- Format: monthly small-group lunches with two other researchers in your city; matching by location, discipline, experience level.
- Geographic focus: **33+ cities globally**.
- Cost: GBP 10 per 3 months (~USD 4/month).

### Ladies that UX Bristol
- URLs: https://www.meetup.com/Ladies-that-UX-Bristol/  ;  https://ladiesthatux.com/bristol/
- Audience: women and underrepresented genders in UX (cis women, trans women, trans men, non-binary).
- Format: talks, fireside chats, career topics; primarily online since the pandemic with some Bristol in-person plans.
- Geographic focus: Bristol, UK (chapter of global Ladies that UX).
- Cost: free.

### UX Research and Strategy
- URL: https://www.uxresearchandstrategy.com/
- Audience: practitioners building UXR and strategy skills.
- Founded 2019 by Jen Blatz, Lauren Singer, Lorie Whitaker.
- Format: workshops and talks; registered US 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
- Topics: landing first UXR job, interview prep, portfolios.
- Geographic focus: US-rooted, virtual reach.
- Cost: free (event-dependent).

### UXPA UK
- URL: https://uxpa-uk.org/
- Audience: UK UX professionals across UCD, research, human factors.
- Format: volunteer-run UK chapter of UXPA International; mentoring; currently on hiatus from hosting events per their site.
- Geographic focus: UK.
- Cost: free chapter participation; UXPA International dues separate.

### Service Design Network (SDN) UK
- URL: https://www.service-design-network.org/chapters/uk
- Audience: service design practitioners, students, academics in the UK.
- Format: UK chapter of global SDN; events including annual SDN UK National Conference; "Service Design YAP" podcast; SDN Insider newsletter.
- Geographic focus: UK.
- Cost: SDN International membership is paid (multiple tiers); chapter events ticketed individually.

### UXinsight Meetup (Netherlands)
- URLs: https://www.meetup.com/UXinsight_meetup/  ;  https://uxinsight.org/
- Audience: UX researchers, designers, PMs.
- Format: online and in-person meetups; runs the annual UXinsight Festival (next: April 20–22, 2026, Leiden, hybrid).
- Geographic focus: Netherlands/European base, global online attendance.
- Cost: meetups generally free; festival and training paid.

## Professional associations

### SIGCHI
- URL: https://sigchi.org/
- Audience: HCI researchers and practitioners (CS, design, psychology, anthropology, etc.).
- Format: ACM Special Interest Group; ~70 active chapters across 6 continents; sponsors CHI and 30 HCI conferences annually.
- Cost: Professional USD 30/year (USD 24 and USD 15 for designated lower-income countries); Student USD 10/year.
- Sign up via ACM membership portal.

### EPIC (epicpeople.org)
- URL: https://www.epicpeople.org/
- Audience: applied ethnographers, researchers, designers, innovators.
- Format: nonprofit member org; annual conference; EPIC Library (450+ articles, 130+ videos); Learning Week; job board; business directory.
- Geographic focus: global.
- Cost: paid annual membership (tiered); discounts on training and conference for members.

### UXPA International
- URL: https://uxpa.org/
- Audience: UX professionals globally.
- Format: professional association; chapters worldwide; flagship annual conference; resources, mentoring program, awards.
- Cost: Professional USD 139/year; Associate USD 69/year; Student USD 35/year.

### Flux (Gated)
- URL: https://www.fluxmember.com/
- Audience: women leading UX and Design teams (manager and above).
- Format: private member network; monthly virtual meet-ups; annual flagship Flux Convergence event at The Barbican, London.
- Geographic focus: UK-rooted, international community.
- Cost: paid membership (price not publicly listed; inquire via info@fluxmember.com); event tickets sold separately.

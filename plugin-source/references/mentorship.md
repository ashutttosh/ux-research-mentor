# Mentorship programs

From the source guide. Each entry: audience, format, geography, cost, how to join.

## Hexagon UX Mentorship
- URL: http://www.hexagonux.com/mentorship
- Audience: womxn and non-binary folx in UX (junior-to-senior pairing).
- Format: **cohort-based, 3 months per cohort**; 2–4 cohorts/year by chapter activity; virtual or in-person; chapter kickoff event required.
- Geographic focus: global chapters (SF, Seattle, Houston, London, Toronto, Paris, etc.).
- Cost: free.
- Portfolio required to apply via chapter pages on hexagonux.com.

## UX Coffee Hours
- URLs: https://uxcoffeehours.com/  (also https://storage.googleapis.com/uxcoffeehours.com/site/research.html)
- Audience: anyone seeking UX mentorship including UXR, PM, design.
- Format: free, 1-hour virtual coffee chats with experienced mentors from major tech companies.
- Cost: free.
- Book a slot on the site with the mentor of your choice.

## SheSays (Who's Your Momma program)
- URL: https://weareshesays.com/
- Audience: women and non-binary people in creative industries; agency/market research/strategy lean.
- Format: global mentorship matching program (Who's Your Momma); events; WEI career platform.
- Geographic focus: global with city chapters (Chicago, AU, London, etc.).
- Cost: free.
- Sign up via chapter pages.

## COOP Careers
- URL: https://coopcareers.org/
- Audience: first-gen or low-income US college grads (bachelor's earned between Aug 2016 and Aug 2026) who are unemployed/underemployed.
- Format: **16-week hybrid fellowship**, ~200 hours, cohorts of 18–20; tracks in Digital Marketing, Data Analytics, Financial Services (no dedicated UXR track).
- Geographic focus: US (NYC, LA, other locations).
- Cost: free.
- Outcomes: cited 75% full-employment rate within 12 months.
- Apply via coopcareers.org; fall 2026 Early Action deadline May 29, 2026.

## ADPList
- URL: https://adplist.org/
- Audience: anyone seeking mentorship in design, product, UX research, AI, engineering, marketing.
- Format: 1:1 and group mentorship; 40,000+ verified mentors total (4,600+ UXR mentors, 6,700+ UX design mentors).
- Cost: free for mentees; mentors volunteer.
- Browse mentors on adplist.org and book directly.
- Comment-suggested mentor: **Aadil Khan** (Anam Chohan: "Free and Well Networked Mentors").

## UXinsight Coaching
- URL: https://uxinsight.org/  (consultancy/coaching subpages)
- Audience: practicing UX researchers seeking growth or guidance.
- Format: **paid** 1-on-1 coaching by experienced UXRs; also team training and consultancy.
- Geographic focus: Netherlands-based with international reach.
- Cost: paid; specific rates not listed publicly (request quote via site).

## Mega Mentor
- URL: https://mega-mentor.com/
- Audience: mid-to-senior designers ready to step into design leadership.
- Format: directory of senior and lead-level design mentors; book via mentors' Calendly links; at least 30-min sessions; rule: no sales or hiring pitches.
- Cost: free.
- Browse mentors and book via their Calendly.

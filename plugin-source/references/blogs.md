# Blogs

The blogs from the source guide, with what each actually covers, audience, and signature content.

## The UX Review
- URL: https://theuxreview.co.uk/ (Medium: https://medium.com/the-ux-review)
- Independent UK publication: articles, interviews, tips across UX basics, strategy, service design, "mind candy" categories.
- Founded and edited by Misato Ehara, London-based UX researcher originally from Nagoya, Japan; formerly a design strategist at Gensler, now researcher at Wellcome Trust.
- Signature format: long-form interviews with UX leaders (Samantha Davies, Mike Atherton, Christina Li, Vikram Singh, Faye Koukia-Koutelaki, Claire Waterworth).
- Audience: emerging and mid-career UX practitioners in Europe wanting to learn from leaders' lived experience.
- Free; no paywall.

## User Interviews Blog
- URL: https://www.userinterviews.com/blog
- Run by User Interviews (participant-recruiting platform, founded 2015; later part of UserTesting).
- Hundreds of articles on UXR methods, strategy, tools, ResearchOps, career.
- Audience: practicing UXRs, ResearchOps, PMs running studies — beginner to senior.
- Signature lines: annual *State of User Research* report, recruitment best practices, career guides, "Best UX Research Blogs" list.
- Free; companion: *Awkward Silences* podcast and webinars.

## Meta Research (Medium + research.facebook.com)
- URLs: https://research.facebook.com/blog/ and https://medium.com/meta-research
- Official Meta research blog covering UX research, HCI, computational social science, applied behavioural research.
- Audience: practitioners interested in mixed-methods, quant UXR, big-tech research practice.
- Signature pieces: Duyen Mary Nguyen's "Research That Counts" on quant UXR; how Meta studies experiences of billions of users.
- Covers methods, career stories from Meta researchers, dedicated research-area pages (HCI & UX).
- Free.

## Condens Magazine (Condens Blog)
- URL: https://condens.io/blog/
- Run by Condens (Germany-based UX research repository and analysis tool).
- Three collections: Research Practice, Research Ops, Collaboration & Impact.
- Audience: UX researchers and ResearchOps leads in teams that need to store, analyse, share insights.
- Topics: research repositories, working with PMs, scaling research, stakeholder management, research-maturity frameworks.
- Also runs "Essence by Condens" series on Medium.
- Free; newsletter signup on site.

## dscout People Nerds
- URL: https://dscout.com/people-nerds
- Run by dscout (remote/diary-study research platform).
- Audience: mixed-methods researchers, especially those running diary studies, longitudinal research, field studies.
- Topic hubs: diary studies, field studies, analysis, AI in UXR, CPG, career and people management.
- Signature pieces: "Ultimate Guide to UX Research Methods," "Upskill with People Nerds" learning path, AI auditing articles.
- Formats: articles, webinars, templates, Q&As, case studies, interviews.
- Free.

## Samantha Davies on Medium / Monzo blog
- URLs: https://sambambo.medium.com/ , https://monzo.com/blog/author/samantha-davies , https://medium.com/monzo-bank
- Author: Samantha Davies — Head of User Research and Design Leadership at Zoopla; previously the first researcher at Monzo, where she built the UXR practice; later Director of UX at Picsart; founding member of Flux.
- Standout posts (Monzo publication): "Why everyone at Monzo does research," "Seven things I learnt scaling research (and design) at Monzo," "The tool we use to turn around user research findings fast at Monzo," "The trick we use to make research findings memorable at Monzo," "Understanding how young people manage their money."
- Audience: UXR managers/leaders scaling research practices in product-led companies.
- Free.

## UX Tools (uxtools.co)
- URLs: https://uxtools.co/ , https://www.uxtools.co/about
- Run by Taylor Palmer and Jordan Bowman (product designers).
- Signature project: annual Design Tools Survey, since 2017, ~400k+ cumulative responses, 25,000+ designers in recent waves; results free forever.
- Content: tutorials, tool comparison databases, design assignments, insider tool news, surveys.
- Audience: product designers and UXRs choosing or comparing software.
- Free; newsletter signup.

## UXinsight Blog
- URLs: https://uxinsight.org/blog/ , resources hub https://uxinsight.org/ux-research-resources/
- Run by UXinsight — largest UX research community in Europe; launched Europe's first UXR conference in 2017.
- Audience: European UXR practitioners + global readers interested in research craft and maturity.
- Signature topics: UX Research Maturity Framework, measuring research impact, AI's effect on UXR, research briefing templates.
- Formats: articles, templates, talk recordings, conference content.
- Companion: UXinsight Festival (next: April 20–22, 2026, Leiden; hybrid). 2026 themes: Re:ground, Re:invent, Re:search.
- Free; monthly newsletter.

## Bootcamp (Medium publication)
- URLs: https://medium.com/design-bootcamp , https://bootcamp.uxdesign.cc/
- Edited by UX Collective Editors; affiliated with UX Collective (uxdesign.cc).
- Audience: designers, UXRs, product folks at all stages — tilt toward newcomers.
- Coverage: UX research methodologies, UI/UX practice, career development, design systems, AI/UX trends.
- Submission-driven with editorial standards; tagged streams for UX Research and UX Design.
- Signature: weekly curated resources roundups by UX Collective Editors.
- Free.
